Commercial Title Insurance

Protection built for higher-stakes transactions.

Commercial real estate often involves significant investment, complex ownership structures, financing requirements, and greater transaction risk. Purple Key provides thorough title review and dependable title insurance designed to protect the investment, reduce risk, and provide confidence from acquisition forward.

What is included

Protection built for higher-stakes transactions.

Entity and authority review

Review of the organizational documents and authority needed for the entity on title to convey or encumber the property.

Survey and ALTA matters

Coordination around survey review and the endorsements a commercial lender or buyer commonly requires.

Multi-party coordination

Working with counsel, lenders, brokers and the other parties a commercial file brings to the table.

Endorsement support

Requests for available endorsements handled with WFG so coverage matches the deal.

Who it is for

Built for the people on the file.

Commercial buyers and sellers, developers, landlords, lenders and the attorneys representing them.
